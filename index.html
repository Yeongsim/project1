Your guide to the best of Nova Scotia's capital city.

Top Attractions

Halifax Waterfront

Stroll along the vibrant boardwalk, enjoy street performers, grab a bite, and visit unique shops. It's the heart of the city's summer activity.

Learn More

Citadel Hill National Historic Site

Step back in time at this historic fort offering panoramic views of the city and harbour. Don't miss the noon gun!

Learn More

Halifax Public Gardens

A Victorian-era garden oasis in the heart of the city, perfect for a relaxing walk amidst beautiful flora and fountains.

Learn More

Maritime Museum of the Atlantic

Explore Nova Scotia's rich maritime history, including exhibits on the Titanic and the Halifax Explosion.

Learn More

Peggy's Cove (Day Trip)

A picturesque fishing village with an iconic lighthouse, just an hour's drive from Halifax. A must-see!

Learn More

Art Gallery of Nova Scotia

Discover regional, national, and international art collections, including the Maud Lewis House exhibit.

Food & Drink

Seafood Delights

Halifax is renowned for its fresh seafood. Don't leave without trying the lobster, scallops, and famous fish and chips. Head to the waterfront for many great options!

The Donair

A Halifax original! This sweet and savory meat wrap is a must-try late-night snack. Find it at many pizza shops around the city.

Outdoors & Nature

Point Pleasant Park

Explore miles of walking trails, historic ruins, and beautiful ocean views in this large forested park at the south end of the Halifax peninsula.

McNabs Island

Take a ferry to this historic island in Halifax Harbour for hiking, exploring old forts, and enjoying pristine beaches.
